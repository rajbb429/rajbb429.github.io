Fart Together – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Fart Together ("the game", "we") is a party game about hippos farting a raft down rivers, played solo or online with friends. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: there are no accounts and no analytics, we don't store anything about you on a server, and the free version shows ads (Plus has none).

What the game uses, and why

Your player name. You can type a name so your friends know which hippo is yours. It is sent to the other players in the same room while you play, and it's saved on your own device so you don't have to type it again. It is not stored anywhere else.

Your voice (only if you turn the mic on). Voice chat is off until you tap the mic button and allow microphone access. While it's on, your voice is streamed live to the other players in your room so they can hear you. It is not recorded or stored, by us or by anyone we use. Tap the mic button again to turn it off. You can also refuse or remove the microphone permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the game works without it.

Game messages. In online games your raft's position, button presses, the room code, your hippo's look (colour, hat and so on) and your name are passed between the players in the room so everyone sees the same race. They are relayed through Supabase Realtime (a messaging service run by Supabase, Inc.) and are not saved: once a message has been delivered it's gone.

Purchases. "Fart Together Plus" is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game only whether you own Plus, so it can unlock the extras. Google's own privacy policy covers the purchase: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ads (free version only). If you don't have Plus, the game shows ads from Google AdMob: ones you choose to watch for a reward (such as trying a Plus river), and now and then one between races. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and how you interact with ads, and may use it to personalise ads where you've agreed. In regions that require it (such as the EU/EEA and the UK) the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Plus players see no ads and the ad service isn't started for them. How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Settings and progress. Your settings (sound, music, controls, your hippo's look) and your stars for each river are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • We don't sell your information, and apart from the ads described above we don't share it.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or files.

Other players

In an online room, the other players can see your name and hear you while your mic is on. Only share your room code with people you want to play with.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. Since we don't store player information, there is nothing kept about any player.

Security

Game messages travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS / secure WebSocket).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
